Research

My doctoral research, advised by Dr. Satish M. Srinivasan, examines AI's role in labor market transformation. I build production ETL pipelines on GCP (BigQuery, Dataflow, Cloud Storage) to collect and analyze longitudinal employer skill demand data, applying BERTopic and LDA to extract occupational skill signals and identify curriculum gaps between what institutions teach and what the labor market actually needs.

A parallel research thread focuses on the digital labor market for spiritual facilitation roles — chaplains, clergy, and pastoral staff. Drawing on IRS, Census, USDA, and SerpAPI data across national, state, and metro architectures, I found an inverse correlation of r = −0.402 (p = 0.038) between digital labor market participation and household income across 27 states, pointing to persistent structural inequities in who benefits from digital labor platforms.

Earlier work on healthcare workforce equity — how clinical AI reshapes the skill profiles of roles from diagnostics to telemedicine — produced a 2025 NABET Best Paper Award co-authored with Dr. Srinivasan. That project also developed an XAI ethics-by-design framework with fairness audits and real-time streaming pipelines.
